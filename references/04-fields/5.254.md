# 5.254 Fixed Radius Transition Indicator

> 缩写 `FIXED RAD IND` ｜ 定义于 424-18 424-19 424-20 424-21 424-22 424-23

**用于：** 4.1.6.1 列 99（Fix Radius Transition Indicator）

## 定义

Definition/Description: Indicates that a specific turn radius from the inbound course to the
outbound course is required by the airspace controlling agency.

Source/Content: When a fix radius turn is required a 3-digit numeric value will be entered in
this field representing the radius of the turn to 1 decimal place (tenths, decimal point
suppressed) in nautical miles. A blank entry in this field indicates that no fixed radius
transition is required. Used On: Enroute Airway Records

Length: 3 characters

Character Type: Numeric Examples: 225=22.5nm, 150=15.0 nm
