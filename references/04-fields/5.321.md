# 5.321 Helipad Maximum Rotor Diameter

> 定义于 424-23 ｜ 缺失于 424-18 424-19 424-20 424-21 424-22

**用于：** 4.1.36.1 列 61（Helipad Maximum Rotor Diameter）；4.2.9.1 列 61（Helipad Maximum Rotor Diameter）

## 定义

Definition/Description: This field defines the maximum rotor diameter allowed for helicopters
operating on the specified pad.

Source/Content: Helipad Maximum Rotor Diameter Pad dimensions will be derived from official
government sources and entered into the field in feet with a resolution of one foot. The field
will be blank when the information is not available. Used On: Airport Helipad and Heliport
Helipad Primary Records

Length: 3 characters

Character Type: Alpha/numeric Examples: 105, 150, 079
