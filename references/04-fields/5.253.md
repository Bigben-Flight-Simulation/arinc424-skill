# 5.253 Primary and Additional Alternate Identifier

> 缩写 `ALT IDENT` ｜ 定义于 424-18 424-19 424-20 424-21 424-22 424-23

**用于：** 4.1.30.1 列 104（Additional Alternate Identifier Five）；4.1.30.1 列 24（Primary Alternate Identifier）；4.1.30.1 列 40（Additional Alternate Identifier One）；4.1.30.1 列 56（Additional Alternate Identifier Two）；4.1.30.1 列 72（Additional Alternate Identifier Three）；4.1.30.1 列 88（Additional Alternate Identifier Four）

## 定义

Definition/Description: The Primary Alternate Identifier and the Additional Alternate
Identifiers (two through five) uniquely identify either an Alternate Airport or an Alternate
Company Route. The determination of whether the content is an Airport Identifier or a Company
Route Identifier is accomplished through the Alternate Type field.

Source/Content: The content of this field is determined by the customer. Used On: Alternate
Records

Length: 10 characters max

Character Type: Alpha/numeric
