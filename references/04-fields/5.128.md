# 5.128 Restrictive Airspace Type

> 缩写 `REST TYPE` ｜ 定义于 424-18 424-19 424-20 424-21 424-22 424-23

**用于：** 4.1.18.1 列 9（Restrictive Type）；4.1.6.3 列 111（Restricted Airspace Type）；4.1.6.3 列 69（Restricted Airspace Type）；4.1.6.3 列 83（Restricted Airspace Type）；4.1.6.3 列 97（Restricted Airspace Type）

## 定义

Definition/Description: The Restrictive Airspace Type field is used to indicate the type of
Airspace in which the flight of aircraft is prohibited or restricted. The restriction may be
continuous or specified for certain times.

Source/Content: The Restrictive Airspace Type should be derived from official government
publications. Used On: Restrictive Airspace and Enroute Airway Flight Planning Continuation
records

Length: 1 character

Character Type: Alpha
