# 5.51 Localizer Width

> 缩写 `LOC WIDTH` ｜ 定义于 424-20 424-21 424-22 424-23 ｜ 缺失于 424-18 424-19

**用于：** 4.1.11.1 列 84（Localizer Width）

## 定义

Definition/Description: The Localizer Width field specifies the localizer course width of the
ILS facility defined in the record.

Source/Content: Localizer course widths from official government sources are entered into the
field in degrees, tenths of a degree and hundredths of a degree with the decimal point
suppressed. Used On: ILS records

Length: 4 characters

Character Type: Numeric Examples: 0500, 0400, 0350
