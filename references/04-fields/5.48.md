# 5.48 Localizer Position (LOC FR RW END) Azimuth/Back Azimuth Position (AZ/BAZ FR

> 定义于 424-18 424-19 424-20 424-21 424-22 424-23

**用于：** 4.1.11.1 列 75（Localizer Position）；4.1.22.1 列 75（Azimuth Position）；4.1.22.2 列 75（Back Azimuth Position）

## 定义

RW END)

Definition/Description: The Localizer/Azimuth Position field defines the location of the
facility antenna relative to one end of the runway.

Source/Content: The field contains the official government source distance, in feet, from the
antenna to the runway end. The resolution is one foot. Used On: ILS, MLS and MLS Continuation
records

Length: 4 characters

Character Type: Numeric Examples: 0950, 1000
