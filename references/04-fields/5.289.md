**用于：** 4.1.34 列 91（Used On）

## 定义

Definition/Description: The Used On field provides an indication of what kind of communications
records a particular Communications Type is used on.

Source/Content: The content will be derived from official government source and will be selected
from the table below. Used On: Communications Type Translation Table Records

Length: 1 character

Character Type: Alpha
