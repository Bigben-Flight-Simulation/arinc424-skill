# 5.285 Time Narrative

> 定义于 424-19 424-20 424-21 424-22 424-23 ｜ 缺失于 424-18

**用于：** 4.1.14.5 列 24（Time Narrative）；4.1.18.3 列 27（Narrative Time）；4.1.21.4 列 23（Narrative Time）；4.1.23.4 列 24（Time Narrative）；4.1.24.4 列 41（Time Narrative）；4.1.25.4 列 27（Time Narrative）；4.1.27.4 列 72（Time Narrative）；4.2.5.5 列 24（Time Narrative）

## 定义

Definition/Description: The Time Narrative field is used to provide Time of Operations and/or
Conditions of Operations in a narrative form when source information cannot be formatted in
accordance with Section 5.195 of this specification.

Source/Content: The field content will be derived from official government sources. Used On:
Enroute/Airport/Heliport Communications Continuation Records, Restrictive, Controlled Airspace
Continuation, Flight Planning Continuation, and Preferred Route Continuation Records

Length: 100 characters max (per record)

Character Type: Alpha/numeric
