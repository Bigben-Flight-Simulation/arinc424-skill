# 5.113 High/Low

> 缩写 `HIGH/LOW` ｜ 定义于 424-18 424-19 424-20 424-21 424-22 424-23

**用于：** 4.1.15.1 列 29（Marker Power）

## 定义

Definition/Description: The High/Low field indicates the power of the enroute marker.

Source/Content: The field contains the power derived from official government sources. The
character L indicates low power for use at low altitudes. The character H indicates high power
for general use. Used On: Enroute marker records

Length: 1 character

Character Type: Alpha
