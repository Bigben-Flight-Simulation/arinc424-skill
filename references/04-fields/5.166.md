# 5.166 Channel

> 定义于 424-18 424-19 424-20 424-21 424-22 424-23

**用于：** 4.1.22.1 列 23（Channel）

## 定义

Definition/Description: The Channel field specifies the channel of the Azimuth, Elevation and
Data transmissions for the MLS identified in the MLS Identifier field of the record.

Source/Content: Channels are derived from official government sources and range from 500 to 699.
Used On: MLS Records

Length: 3 characters

Character Type: Numeric
