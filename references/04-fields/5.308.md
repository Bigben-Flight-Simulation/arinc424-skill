# 5.308 Remote Altimeter Flag

> 定义于 424-21 424-22 424-23 ｜ 缺失于 424-18 424-19 424-20

**用于：** 4.1.9.5 列 74（Remote Altimeter Flag）；4.2.3.5 列 74（Remote Altimeter Flag）

## 定义

Definition/Description: The field indicates whether or not the existence and use of a Remote
Altimeter Setting is applicable to the procedure with the meaning that LNAV/VNAV (Baro-VNAV) is
Not Authorized with the Remote Altimeter Setting is being used.

Source/Content: The field content is based on government sources. The field contains the
character R when there is a Remote Altimeter Restriction on the use of LNAV/VNAV (Baro-VNAV)
Lines of Minimum. For all other cases, the field is blank. Used On: Procedure Data Continuation
Records

Length: 1 Character

Character Type: Alpha
