# 5.86 Cruise Altitude

> 定义于 424-18 424-20 424-21 424-22 424-23 ｜ 缺失于 424-19

**用于：** 4.1.12.1 列 73（Cruise Altitude）；4.2.7.1 列 88（Cruise Altitude）

## 定义

Definition/Description: This field will be used to establish an Enroute Cruise Altitude. It will
be entered on Company Route records as specified by the customer.

Source/Content: The customer will supply the Cruise Attitude in feet or flight level. Used On:
Company Route, Helicopter Operations Company Route Records

Length: 5 characters

Character Type: Alpha/numeric Examples: 10000, 15000, FL090, FL240
