# 5.62 Inbound Holding Course

> 缩写 `IB HOLD CRS` ｜ 定义于 424-18 424-19 424-20 424-21 424-22 424-23

**用于：** 4.1.5.1 列 40（Inbound Holding Course）

## 定义

Definition/Description: The Inbound Holding Course field defines the inbound course to the
holding waypoint.

Source/Content: Inbound holding courses derived from official government sources are entered
into the field in degrees and tenths of a degree, with the decimal point suppressed. For holding
courses published with true bearings, the holding pattern true. magnetic variation must be coded
as Used On: Holding Pattern records

Length: 4 characters

Character Type: Numeric Examples: 0456, 1800, 3034
