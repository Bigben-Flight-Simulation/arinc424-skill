# 5.109 Runway Width

> 缩写 `WIDTH` ｜ 定义于 424-18 424-19 424-20 424-21 424-22 424-23

**用于：** 4.1.10.1 列 77（Runway Width）

## 定义

Definition/Description: The width of the runway identified in the Runway Identifier field is
specified in the Runway Width field.

Source/Content: Runway widths derived from Official Government Sources are entered into the
field in feet, with a resolution of one foot. For runways of variable width, the minimum width
encountered over the runway length will be entered. Used On: Runway records

Length: characters

Character Type: Numeric Examples: 0150, 0300, 0075,
