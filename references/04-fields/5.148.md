# 5.148 Enroute Alternate Airport/Heliport

> 缩写 `EAA` ｜ 定义于 424-18 424-19 424-20 424-21 424-22 424-23

**用于：** 4.1.12.1 列 91（Enroute Alternate Airport）；4.2.7.1 列 108（Enroute Alternate Heliport）

## 定义

Definition/Description: The Enroute Alternate Airport/Heliport field identifies the most
suitable emergency airport or heliport along a Company Route or Helicopter Operations Company
Route.

Source/Content: This field is determined by the user airline and will contain the Airport or
Heliport Ident. Used On: Company Route, Helicopter Operations Company Route records

Length: 4 characters

Character Type: Alpha/numeric Examples: KDEN, EGKK, EDFF
