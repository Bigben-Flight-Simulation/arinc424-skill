# 5.22 Turn Direction Valid

> 缩写 `TDV` ｜ 定义于 424-18 424-19 424-20 424-21 424-22 424-23

**用于：** 4.1.9.1 列 50（Turn Direction Valid）；4.2.3.1 列 50（Turn Direction Valid）

## 定义

Definition/Description: This field is used in conjunction with Turn direction to indicate that a
turn is required prior to capturing the path defined in a terminal procedure.

Source/Content: The field contains the alpha character Y when a turn is required prior to
beginning the leg defined by the Path Term. The direction of the turn is specified in Section
5.20. Used On: Airport and Heliport SID/STAR/Approach Records

Length: 1 character

Character Type: Alpha
