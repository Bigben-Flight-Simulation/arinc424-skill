# 5.56 Gate Identifier

> 缩写 `GATE IDENT` ｜ 定义于 424-18 424-19 424-20 424-21 424-22 424-23

**用于：** 4.1.8.1 列 14（Gate Identifier）

## 定义

Definition/Description: The airport gate defined in the record is identified in the Gate
Identifier field.

Source/Content: Coded gate identity information is derived from official government sources and
navigation system users. Used On: Gate records

Length: 5 characters max Character Type Alpha/numeric Examples: C134B, 23, 30A, B12A
