# 5.322 Helipad Type

> 定义于 424-23 ｜ 缺失于 424-18 424-19 424-20 424-21 424-22

**用于：** 4.1.36.1 列 64（Helipad Type）；4.2.9.1 列 64（Helipad Type）

## 定义

Definition/Description: This field provides information if the Helipad is located at an elevated
location, e.g., rooftop.

Source/Content: The Helipad Type will be derived from official government sources. It will be
selected from the table below: Used On: Airport Helipad and Heliport Helipad Primary Records

Length: 1 character

Character Type: Alpha/numeric

## 正文表格

（424-23 第 322 页）

| Helipad Type Field | Content |
|---|---|
| Elevated | E |
| Other or unknown | Blank |
