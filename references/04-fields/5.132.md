# 5.132 NOTAM

> 定义于 424-18 424-19 424-20 424-21 424-22 424-23

**用于：** 4.1.14.1 列 118（NOTAM）；4.1.14.4 列 25（NOTAM）；4.1.18.1 列 28（NOTAM）；4.1.18.2 列 28（NOTAM）；4.1.23.2 列 68（NOTAM）；4.1.23.3 列 25（NOTAM）；4.1.25.1 列 28（NOTAM）；4.1.25.2 列 28（NOTAM）；4.2.5.1 列 118（NOTAM）；4.2.5.4 列 25（NOTAM）

## 定义

Definition/Description: Restrictive Airspace areas and Communication facilitiies may not have
established active times and are activated by NOTAM or may be active by NOTAM in addition to
established times.

Source/Content: Active times by NOTAM will be derived from official government source. When used
on airspace primary records, the area is active only by NOTAM and there will be no continuation
record. When used on continuation airspace records, the area is active by NOTAM in addition to
the established times. When used on communications primary extension continuation records, the
communication facility may be active by NOTAM only or by NOTAM in addition to formatted times of
operation. The field will contain the alpha character N to indicate either condition, otherwise
the field will be blank. Used On: Controlled Airspace Records, Primary and Continuation
Restrictive Airspace Continuation records, Primary and and Airport/Heliport/Enroute
Communication Primary Extension Records.

Length: 1 character

Character Type: Alpha
