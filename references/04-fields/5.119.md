# 5.119 Arc Distance

> 缩写 `ARC DIST` ｜ 定义于 424-18 424-19 424-20 424-21 424-22 424-23

**用于：** 4.1.17.1 列 73（Arc Distance）；4.1.18.1 列 71（Arc Distance）；4.1.25.1 列 71（Arc Distance）

## 定义

Definition/Description: The Arc Distance field is used to define the distance in nautical miles
from the Arc Origin position to the arc defining the lateral boundary of a FIR/UIR or
Restrictive Airspace.

Source/Content: ARC distances should be derived from official government sources when available,
in nautical miles and tenths of nautical mile, with the decimal point suppressed. The field will
be entered only when Boundary Via is A, C, L, or R. Used On: FIR/UIR, Restrictive Airspace, and
Controlled Airspace records

Length: 4 characters

Character Type: Numeric Examples: 0080, 0150, 1000
