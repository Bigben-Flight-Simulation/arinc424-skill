# 5.69 Threshold Displacement Distance

> 缩写 `DSPLCD THR` ｜ 定义于 424-18 424-19 424-20 424-21 424-22 424-23

**用于：** 4.1.10.1 列 72（Displaced Threshold Distance）

## 定义

Definition/Description: The threshold displacement distance indicates the distance from the
extremity of a runway to the landing threshold (Section 2.2.3) not located at that extremity of
that runway. The threshold displacement distance also serves as a flag which indicates if the
landing threshold is displaced or not; a distance value greater than zero indicates the landing
threshold is a displaced threshold, and a lack of a value indicates the coordinates are for the
extremity of the runway.

Source/Content: Threshold displacement distances derived from official government sources are
entered into this field in feet, foot. to a resolution of 1 Used On: Runway records

Length: 4 characters

Character Type: Numeric Examples: 0485, 1260
