# 5.46 Runway Identifier

> 缩写 `RUNWAY ID` ｜ 定义于 424-18 424-19 424-20 424-21 424-22 424-23

**用于：** 4.1.10.1 列 14（Runway Identifier）；4.1.11.1 列 28（Runway or Helipad Identifier）；4.1.13.1 列 28（Runway Helipad Identifier）；4.1.22.1 列 28（Runway Identifier）；4.1.28.1 列 20（Runway Identifier or Final Approach Course as Runway）；4.1.29.1 列 28（Runway or Helipad Identifier）；4.1.35.1 列 20（Runway Identifier or Final Approach Course as Runway）；4.2.8.1 列 20（Runway Indentifier or Final Approach Course As Runway）

## 定义

Definition/Description: The Runway Identifier field identifies the runways described in runway
records and runways served by the ILS/MLS described in ILS/MLS records.

Source/Content: Runway identifiers are derived from official government sources and are shown in
the following format: The two letters RW are followed by two numeric, 01 thru 36, and may
contain a fifth character designation of one of the following: Any other designations
(suffixes), such as North, South, East, West, True, or STOL will not be included in the ARINC
424 database file. Used On: Airport and Heliport ILS and MLS, GLS Runway, Airport and Heliport
Localizer Marker, Path Point, and GLS Records.

Length: 5 characters max

Character Type: Alpha/numeric Examples: RW26L, RW08R, RW26C, RW05, RW02W, RW12G
