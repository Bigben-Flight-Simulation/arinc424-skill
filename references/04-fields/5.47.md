# 5.47 Localizer Bearing

> 缩写 `LOC BRG` ｜ 定义于 424-18 424-20 424-21 424-22 424-23 ｜ 缺失于 424-19

**用于：** 4.1.11.1 列 52（Localizer Bearing）；4.1.29.1 列 52（GLS Approach Bearing）

## 定义

Definition/Description: The Localizer Bearing field defines the bearing of the localizer course
of the ILS facility/GLS approach described in the record.

Source/Content: Localizer courses, derived from official government sources, are entered into
the field in degrees and tenths of a degree, with the decimal point suppressed. For localizer
courses published with the intent to be used as true courses, the Government Source field (5.95)
must be set to T and localizer station declination or GLS magnetic variation must be set to
true. Used On: ILS, GLS Records

Length: 4 characters

Character Type: Numeric Examples: 2570, 0147, 2910
