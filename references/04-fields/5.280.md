# 5.280 Special Activity Area Size

> 定义于 424-19 424-20 424-21 424-22 424-23 ｜ 缺失于 424-18

**用于：** 4.1.33.1 列 43（SAA Size）

## 定义

Definition/Description: The Special Activity Area Size field contains the radius around the
center point where the Special Activity is expected to occur.

Source/Content: The Special Activity Area Size is to be defined from official government
publications when available. The radius is entered in nautical miles to a tenth of a nautical
mile with the decimal point suppressed. Used On: Special Activity Area records.

Length: 3 characters

Character Type: Numeric Examples: 020, 105, 050
