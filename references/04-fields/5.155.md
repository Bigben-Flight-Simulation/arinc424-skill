# 5.155 BARO-VNAV Not Authorized

> 定义于 424-18 424-20 424-21 424-22 424-23 ｜ 缺失于 424-19

**用于：** 4.1.9.5 列 75（Baro-VNAV Not Authorized）；4.2.3.5 列 75（Baro VNAV Not Authorized）

## 定义

Definition/Description: The field indicates if the use of Baro-VNAV is not authorized for the
LNAV/VNAV Level of Service.

Source/Content: The field content is based on government sources. The field contains the
character X when Baro-VNAV is not authorized for the LNAV/VNAV Line of Minima. For all other
cases, the field is blank. Used On: Procedure Data Continuation Records

Length: 1 Character

Character Type: Alpha/numeric
