# 5.248 Station Elevation WGS84

> 定义于 424-18 424-19 424-20 424-21 424-22 424-23

**用于：** 4.1.29.1 列 111（Station Elevation WGS 84）

## 定义

Description/Definition: This field identifies the WGS84 elevation of the GLS ground station
described in the record.

Source/Content: The value for this field will be derived from official government sources or
entered into this field in feet with respect to the WGS84 ellipsoid. When elevation is below the
WGS 84 ellipsoid, the first column of the field contains a minus (-) sign. Used On: GLS Record

Length: 5 characters

Character Type: Alpha/numeric Examples: 00530, -0140
