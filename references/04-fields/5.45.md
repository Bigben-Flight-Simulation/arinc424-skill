# 5.45 Localizer Frequency

> 缩写 `FREQ` ｜ 定义于 424-18 424-20 424-21 424-22 424-23 ｜ 缺失于 424-19

**用于：** 4.1.11.1 列 23（Localizer Frequency）

## 定义

Definition/Description: The Localizer Frequency field specifies the VHF frequency of the
facility identified in the Localizer Identifier field.

Source/Content: The official government-source localizer frequency is entered into the field
with a resolution of 50 kHz. The decimal point following the unit MHz entry is suppressed. Used
On: Airport and Heliport ILS Localizer records

Length: 5 characters

Character Type: Numeric Examples: 11030, 11195
