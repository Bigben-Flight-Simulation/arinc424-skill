# 5.60 Name

> 缩写 `NAME` ｜ 定义于 424-18 424-19 424-20 424-21 424-22 424-23

**用于：** 4.1.5.1 列 99（Name）；4.1.8.1 列 99（Name）

## 定义

Definition/Description: The Name field defines the name commonly applied to the navigation
entity defined in the record.

Source/Content: Appropriate contents for the field will be determined from official government
or customer sources. Used On: Gate and Holding Pattern records

Length: 25 characters max

Character Type: Alpha/numeric Examples: HOLDING JIMEE MIAMI
