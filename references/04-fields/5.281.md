# 5.281 Special Activity Area Volume

> 定义于 424-19 424-20 424-21 424-22 424-23 ｜ 缺失于 424-18

**用于：** 4.1.33.1 列 53（SAA Volume）

## 定义

Definition/Description: The Special Activity Area Volume field contains the expected annual
level of intensity of the Special Activity.

Source/Content: The Special Activity Area Volume is to be derived from official government
publications when available. Used On: Special Activity Area records

Length: 1 character

Character Type: Alpha/numeric
