# 5.282 Special Activity Area Operating Times

> 定义于 424-19 424-20 424-21 424-22 424-23 ｜ 缺失于 424-18

**用于：** 4.1.33.1 列 54（Operating Times）

## 定义

Definition/Description: The Special Activity Area Operating Times field contains the annual
expected operation schedule of the Special Activity.

Source/Content: The Special Activity Area Operating Times is to be derived from official
government publications when available. Used On: Special Activity Area records

Length: 3 characters

Character Type: Alpha Examples: DXD (Weekdays, Excluding Holidays from Sunrise to Sunset)
