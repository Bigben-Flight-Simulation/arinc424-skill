# 5.115 Directional Restriction

> 定义于 424-18 424-19 424-20 424-21 424-22 424-23

**用于：** 4.1.24.1 列 94（Direction Restriction）；4.1.6.1 列 47（Direction Restriction）

## 定义

Definition/Description: The Direction Restriction field, when used on Enroute Airway records,
will indicate the direction an Enroute Airway is to be flown. The Direction Restriction field,
when used on Preferred Route records, will indicate whether the routing is available only in the
direction of from initial fix to terminus fix or in both directions.

Source/Content: Direction Restrictions should be derived from official government sources. They
will be coded and supplied as follows: Enroute Airway Records Preferred Route Records Used On:
Enroute Airway and Preferred Route Records

Length: 1 character

Character Type: Alpha

## 码表

> 取自 `arinc424-core` 的枚举，比正文表格更完整；变体名即语义。

**取值** — `DirectionRestrictionItem`

| 代码 | 含义 |
|---|---|
| ` ` | Blank |
| `F` | Forward |
| `B` | Backward |

## 正文表格

（424-23 第 233 页）

| F One way in direction route is coded (Forward). |
|---|
| B One way in opposite direction route is coded (backward). |
| Blank No restrictions on direction. |

（424-23 第 233 页）

| F Uni-directional Preferred Route, usable only from Initial Fix to Terminus | Fix. |
|---|---|
| B Bi-directional Preferred Route, usable from Initial Fix to Terminus Fix or from Terminus Fix to Initial | Fix. |
