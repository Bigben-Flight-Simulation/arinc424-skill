# 5.112 Marker Shape

> 缩写 `SHAPE` ｜ 定义于 424-18 424-19 424-20 424-21 424-22 424-23

**用于：** 4.1.15.1 列 28（Marker Shape）

## 定义

Definition/Description: The Shape field defines the radiation pattern of an airways marker as
being either bone or elliptical.

Source/Content: The field contains the shape of the marker derived from official government
sources when available. The character B will designate the bone shape and the character E will
designate the elliptical shape. E will be entered when the source does not supply shape
information. Used On: Enroute airways marker records

Length: 1 character

Character Type: Alpha
