# 5.117 FIR/UIR Indicator

> 缩写 `IND` ｜ 定义于 424-18 424-19 424-20 424-21 424-22 424-23

**用于：** 4.1.17.1 列 15（FIR/UIR Indicator）；4.1.23.1 列 15（Indicator）

## 定义

Definition/Description: When used on Enroute Communications Records, the content definition
above for the FIR/UIR Record is to be applied whenever the FIR/RDO (5.190) field of the Enroute
Communications Record contains an Information Region Identifier. In all other cases, the
Indicator field of the Enroute Communications Record will be blank.

Source/Content: Used On: FIR/UIR and Enroute Communications records

Length: 1 character

Character Type: Alpha

## 正文表格

（424-23 第 234 页）

| UIR | U |
| Combined FIR/UIR | B |
