# 5.250 Alternate Record Type

> 缩写 `ART` ｜ 定义于 424-18 424-19 424-20 424-21 424-22 424-23

**用于：** 4.1.30.1 列 16（Alternate Record Type）

## 定义

Definition/Description: The Alternate Record Type field identifiers the record as being
applicable to the departure airport (take-off alternate), destination airport (arrival
alternate) or a fix along the route (enroute alternate).

Source/Content: The Alternate Record Type will be selected from the following table: Used On:
Alternate Records

Length: 2 characters

Character Type: Alpha
