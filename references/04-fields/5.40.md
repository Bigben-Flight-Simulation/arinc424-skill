# 5.40 DME Elevation

> 缩写 `DME ELEV` ｜ 定义于 424-18 424-19 424-20 424-21 424-22 424-23

**用于：** 4.1.2.1 列 80（DME Elevation）；4.1.32.1 列 80（TACAN Elevation）

## 定义

Definition/Description: The DME Elevation field defines the elevation of the DME component of
the NAVAID described in the record.

Source/Content: DME elevations specified in official government publications are entered into
this field in feet with respect to MSL. When the elevation is below MSL, the first column of the
field contains a minus (-) sign. Used On: VHF NAVAID records

Length: 5 characters

Character Type: Alpha/numeric Examples: 00530, -0140
